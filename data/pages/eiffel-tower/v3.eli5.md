# Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is a big metal tower. People see it in pictures of Paris.

## Where it stands

The Eiffel Tower is in Paris, France [#28].

It is a famous spot in the city.

## How tall it is

The Eiffel Tower is taller than 300 metres [#15].

It stands high above the streets.

It is like a giant ladder to the sky.
