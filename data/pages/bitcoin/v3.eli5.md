# Bitcoin

Bitcoin is computer money. People can send it on the internet.

## A limited supply
Every four years, Bitcoin makes half as much new bitcoin [#10].
There will only ever be 21 million bitcoin [#11].
This makes bitcoin hard to get. It is like a special treasure chest.

## A public ledger
The first Bitcoin block was made in January 2009 [#19].
That block started a big list everyone can see.
