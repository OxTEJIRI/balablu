# Bitcoin

Bitcoin is a digital money system that people can send to each other over the internet.

## A limited supply
Bitcoin's new supply is cut in half about every four years [#10].
There will never be more than 21 million bitcoin [#11].
This makes bitcoin feel scarce, like a fixed treasure chest.

## A public ledger
The first Bitcoin block was mined in January 2009 [#19].
That block started a public record of bitcoin transactions.
