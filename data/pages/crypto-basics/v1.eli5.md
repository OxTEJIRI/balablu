# Crypto Basics

Crypto is a kind of computer money.
It is easier to understand when you keep coins and tools apart.
Coins are one thing.
Tools are another thing.

## Wallets and payments
Some people say a wallet app keeps your coins inside it. [#25]
That is not true.
A wallet app is like a key ring for your coins.
It helps you keep keys.
The keys let you use your coins.
Your coins are on a big shared list.
That list is called a blockchain.
Some people say you can undo money sent to the wrong computer address. [#26]
That is not true.
