# The Great Wall

The Great Wall is a famous wall in the north of China. It is many walls built to keep people safe. It goes across the land.

## A Moon-view myth
Some people tell a funny story. They say you can see the Great Wall from the Moon. [#4]
That is not true. The wall is too small and thin to see from the Moon.

## A very long wall
All its parts together are more than 20,000 km long. [#5]
It has many walls. It also uses hills and other natural things to help.
