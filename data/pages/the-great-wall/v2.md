# The Great Wall

The Great Wall is a famous set of fortifications that winds across northern China.

## A Moon-view myth
A popular myth says that you can see the Great Wall of China from the Moon with your naked eye. [#4]
It is too thin and small to be seen from the Moon without help.

## A very long wall
The Great Wall's sections add up to more than 20,000 km. [#5]
It includes many walls and natural barriers.
