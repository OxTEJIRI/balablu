# NEAR Protocol

NEAR is a computer network. It shares work. It helps coins move between networks.

## People and launch
Some people say the real NEAR network started in 2018. [#0]
That is not true.
It started later.
Illia Polosukhin helped start NEAR.
He helped write a big paper in 2017. [#1]
The paper was about the Transformer.

## Design and governance
Sharding is like friends sharing a big job.
NEAR uses a sharing plan called Nightshade. [#23]
In October 2020, the community began to lead NEAR. [#2]

## Swaps
Swaps between networks can help coins move.
NEAR Intents helps people trade coins on different networks. [#24]
Some people say NEAR Intents traded more than $10 billion. [#3]
