# NEAR Protocol

NEAR Protocol is a blockchain project known for its sharded design and cross-chain swap tools.

## People and launch
A popular myth says that NEAR mainnet launched in 2018. [#0]
NEAR mainnet launched after 2018.
NEAR co-founder Illia Polosukhin co-wrote the 2017 paper that introduced the Transformer. [#1]

## Design and governance
Sharding splits work across parts of a network.
NEAR uses a sharded design called Nightshade. [#23]
NEAR became community-governed in October 2020. [#2]

## Swaps
Cross-chain swaps can make moving assets easier.
NEAR Intents lets people swap coins across different blockchains. [#24]
Some say that NEAR Intents has processed more than $10 billion in swaps. [#3]
