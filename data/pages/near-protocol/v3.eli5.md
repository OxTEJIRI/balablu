# NEAR Protocol

NEAR Protocol is a big computer system. People use it to share things and trade things. It is like a big shared playground. It started with early internet ideas. People run it together. Later, it got money stuff too.

## Origins
Some people tell a story. They say NEAR started in 2018. [#0] That is not true. NEAR started later.

## People and ideas
Illia Polosukhin helped start NEAR. He helped write a 2017 paper about a computer idea. [#1]

## Community and activity
In October 2020, the NEAR community started making big choices. [#2] Some people say NEAR Intents traded more than $10 billion. [#3]
