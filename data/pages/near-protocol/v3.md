# NEAR Protocol

NEAR Protocol is a blockchain project with a story that mixes early internet ideas, community control, and later finance activity.

## Origins
A popular myth says that NEAR mainnet launched in 2018. [#0]
NEAR mainnet launched later.

## People and ideas
NEAR co-founder Illia Polosukhin co-wrote the 2017 paper that introduced the Transformer. [#1]

## Community and activity
NEAR became community-governed in October 2020. [#2]
Some say that NEAR Intents has processed more than $10 billion in swaps. [#3]
