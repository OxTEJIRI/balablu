BalaBlu: the encyclopedia where every fact has odds

BalaBlu is an encyclopedia where every sentence that can be true or false has live odds. People call a claim FACT or BALABLU (nonsense) with free practice points or a few USDC. A jury of three AI models, each running inside a sealed NEAR AI enclave, settles claims at surprise times. Each vote is signed by the enclave, and a NEAR smart contract verifies the signatures itself before it pays anyone. This is a testnet demo: points and test USDC have no real value.
